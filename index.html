Liner notes for the city

Buses lead the song

About 4,000 BMTC buses report where they are every few minutes. When one in your area reports, it plays: the veena if it's moving, the bansuri flute if it's standing still. You hear them about a minute after they happen, in the order they happened.

Traffic sets the pulse

On Side A a kanjira and mridangam keep Adi tala. Flip to Side B for Carnatic jazz: walking bass, keys built from the raga's notes, and a swinging ride cymbal. Either way the tempo follows your area's buses, so a jam at Silk Board slows the whole song down.

The metro adds colour

Trains follow the BMRCL timetable, not live GPS. When one reaches a station in your area, the Purple Line plays santoor, Green plays jal tarang and Yellow plays temple bells.

Every area has a raga

Each area has its own five-note raga, from Hindolam in Jayanagar to Mohanam in HSR Layout, with a tanpura tuned to match. Move the needle and the whole ensemble retunes.
